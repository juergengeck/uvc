# EN 17141 Biocontamination-Control Evidence PRD

## Status

Status: requirements baseline; implementation planned across
`@refinio/biocontamination.core` and `@refinio/uvc.core`.

Canonical knowledge book: `EN17141.book`.

This PRD defines the product and data boundary required for UVC to support a
facility's EN 17141 microbiological contamination-control program. It does not
claim that UVC, a UVC cycle, or this software is independently "EN 17141
compliant."

The authored `one/EN17141.book` folder in this repository owns this PRD and its
supporting evidence-model, control-logic, architecture, and validation
documents. VGER consumes those tracked documents as source material and may
materialize them into `source.core` `Source`, `SourceEntry`, `SourceRun`,
`BookProjection`, `Book`, and `Library` objects. The authored documents and the
runtime objects are two layers of one Book lifecycle; neither is a copy of the
licensed standard.

## Normative basis

The primary standard is:

- EN 17141:2020, *Cleanrooms and associated controlled environments —
  Biocontamination control*
- DIN EN 17141:2021-02 for the German adoption

Public records and previews:

- https://knowledge.bsigroup.com/products/cleanrooms-and-associated-controlled-environments-biocontamination-control
- https://www.dinmedia.de/de/norm/din-en-17141/312457370
- https://preview.sist.si/sist-preview/61113/8b7b114eb33f4e8b8e51a809d01c6154/SIST-EN-17141-2020.pdf

The deploying organization shall use a licensed copy of the applicable
standard and identify all additional local regulatory and quality-system
requirements.

EN 17141 is limited to viable microbiological contamination in clean controlled
environments. It excludes endotoxin, prion/proteinaceous infectious material,
and viral contamination. A general hospital room, treatment room, bathroom, or
operating room is not automatically in scope. Applicability must be declared by
the responsible organization for each controlled environment.

UV-C product and human-exposure safety are parallel requirements, not EN 17141
evidence. The safety lane shall consider the applicable editions of ISO 15858
and IEC 62471-6 and any product-, workplace-, or jurisdiction-specific rules:

- https://www.iso.org/standard/55553.html
- https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/59543

## Problem

The current product has a strong authenticated device-control and evidence
foundation:

- `UvcControlCommand` records issuer, executor, target, desired state, and time.
- `UvcControlObservation` references the exact command and records the
  producer, observed state or failure, and time.
- `UvcDisinfectionRun` records room, resources, lifecycle times, optional dose,
  and exact start/stop observation hashes.
- A run cannot become `running` without observed start evidence.
- A run cannot become `completed` without observed stop evidence.
- Groov writes require Manage readback and ESP32 writes require correlated
  device state.
- The producer-owned `UvcStateTrie` and CHUM graph preserve typed references
  without reconstructing state from scans.

This proves only that an authenticated device authority observed configured
resources being enabled and later disabled. It does not prove:

- that EN 17141 applies to the room;
- that a formal microbiological contamination-control system exists;
- that contamination sources, routes, risks, or microorganisms of interest
  were assessed;
- that an approved UVC procedure was applicable at the time of the cycle;
- that a calibrated germicidal dose reached required locations;
- that required safety and exclusion conditions were continuously satisfied;
- that viable microbiological contamination was sampled or reduced;
- that alert/action limits were met;
- that an excursion was investigated or a CAPA was effective;
- that qualified personnel reviewed and approved the evidence.

The current Journal projection overstates the evidence by rendering a completed
device cycle as `Disinfection completed`. The software must distinguish device
execution, delivered physical dose, microbiological effectiveness, and program
conformance.

## Product position

UVC shall be an evidence system for a facility-owned microbiological
contamination-control program.

UVC may represent UV-C as one approved control method or controlled
intervention within that program. UV-C telemetry shall never be converted
directly into a viable microbiological result or a standards-conformance claim.

The product claim is:

> UVC preserves attributable evidence connecting an approved contamination
> control program, controlled interventions, microbiological monitoring,
> excursions, actions, and qualified review.

The product claim is not:

> A completed UVC cycle makes a room EN 17141 compliant, sterile, disinfected,
> or microbiologically acceptable.

## Goals

- Represent facility-specific applicability of EN 17141.
- Represent the approved formal microbiological contamination-control system.
- Bind every observation to the exact program, environment, risk, plan, method,
  limit, procedure, equipment, and qualification revisions applicable when the
  observation was produced.
- Preserve contamination sources, transfer routes, risks, mitigations, residual
  risks, and microorganisms of interest.
- Represent environmental monitoring locations, schedules, samples, custody,
  methods, equipment status, incubation, enumeration, identification, and raw
  results.
- Evaluate results against the exact effective-dated limits bound to the sample.
- Detect and preserve excursions without hiding or auto-resolving them.
- Represent investigations, containment, CAPA, effectiveness checks, and
  authorized closure.
- Record UV-C plans and executions without inflating device evidence into
  microbiological effectiveness.
- Produce traceable trend and periodic review evidence.
- Share the typed evidence graph through existing ONE/CHUM mechanisms.
- Make incomplete, missing, expired, superseded, or contradictory evidence
  visible and fail closed for controlled operations.

## Non-goals

- Reproducing the licensed standard.
- Certifying a facility, product, device, process, or organization.
- Defining universal microbial limits, sampling frequencies, organisms,
  germicidal doses, or acceptance criteria.
- Inferring applicability from `UvcRoom.roomKind`, room names, device types, or
  facility labels.
- Treating particle counts, UV-C telemetry, device state, or generic sensor
  values as viable microbiological observations.
- Claiming viral-control evidence under EN 17141.
- Replacing validated laboratory, sampling, calibration, training, quality,
  safety, or change-control processes.
- Adding a single `compliant` boolean.
- Adding JSON compliance blobs, free-form journal-only evidence, startup scans,
  or downstream provenance reconstruction.
- Automatically retrying physical writes or silently accepting missing
  readback.

## Users and authorities

- **Program owner:** organization or accountable Person that owns the formal
  microbiological contamination-control program.
- **Quality approver:** authorized Person who approves applicability, plans,
  procedures, limits, changes, investigations, and closure.
- **Qualified microbiologist:** Person authorized to review methods, results,
  trends, excursions, and effectiveness.
- **Sampling operator:** qualified Person who collects and transfers samples.
- **UVC operator:** qualified Person who plans or initiates an approved UVC
  intervention.
- **Device authority:** authenticated Groov or ESP32 peer producing command and
  readback evidence.
- **Laboratory producer:** identified Person/Instance or validated integration
  that produces microbiological observations and results.
- **Auditor/reviewer:** authorized read-only participant who follows exact
  evidence references.

Identity, pairing, or an admin role does not imply microbiological competence.
Every controlled activity shall resolve a current qualification and scope.

## Required claim vocabulary

The product shall keep these states separate:

### Device-cycle state

- `planned`
- `running`
- `executed`
- `failed`
- `cancelled`

`executed` means that the device execution record is complete. It does not mean
effective disinfection.

### Physical-delivery assessment

- `not-assessed`
- `pending`
- `meets-procedure`
- `deviates-from-procedure`
- `inconclusive`

This assessment concerns wavelength, configuration, irradiance/dose,
distribution, exposure time, safety state, and other approved procedure
criteria. It is not a microbiological result.

### Microbiological assessment

- `not-assessed`
- `pending`
- `acceptable`
- `alert-excursion`
- `action-excursion`
- `out-of-specification`
- `inconclusive`

### Program review state

- `draft`
- `approved`
- `effective`
- `superseded`
- `withdrawn`
- `expired`

No projection may collapse these state families into `compliant`.

| 4.4 Sources and transfer routes | Physical room/resources only | Typed sources, airborne/surface/liquid routes, processes, people, materials, equipment, and microorganisms of interest |
| 4.5 Risk assessment | None | Versioned risk assessment with rationale, mitigation, residual risk, approval, review date, and change linkage |
| 4.6 Monitoring plan | None | Approved plan with locations, frequency/schedule, methods, operating states, sample types, and required actions |
| 4.7 Alert/action limits | None | Effective-dated, method/location/state-specific target, alert, action, and specification limits |

| 4.9 Personnel education/training | Identity and device admin authorization | Qualification scopes, evidence, effective/expiry dates, suspension, and retraining |
| 5.1 Trending | Calendar/run listing | Method- and state-aware viable result series, trend rules, review, and referenced conclusions |

## Architecture

### Domain ownership

`@refinio/biocontamination.core` shall own the reusable EN 17141 quality
domain:

- program, applicability, controlled-environment, risk, monitoring, sampling,
  microbiology, limit, excursion, investigation, CAPA, trend, and review
  object interfaces and recipes;
- creator validation and normalization;
- `BiocontaminationPlan`;
- result evaluation, excursion, trend, and program-review logic;
- program-scoped trie paths and root identifiers;
- the public `biocontamination` operation types;
- tests of object and plan invariants.

`@refinio/uvc.core` shall own only the UV-C intervention domain:

- treatment configuration, procedure, safety, dose, intervention plan,
  authorization attempt, execution, deviation, and physical-delivery
  assessment objects;
- `UvcControlPlan`, `UvcInterventionService`, and the `uvc-intervention`
  operation;
- the adapter that binds exact UVC evidence into `BiocontaminationPlan`.

Expo, Cube, Browser, and Groov shall consume these same domain plans through
their supplied module dependencies. UI surfaces shall consume projections and
operations; they shall not implement quality or intervention decisions.

The hardware control path remains owned by `UvcControlPlan`.
`BiocontaminationPlan` references producer-owned UVC commands, observations,
executions, and assessments rather than copying or reinterpreting them.

### Inventory and supply boundary

Inventory or supply objects may identify equipment, media, consumable lots,
materials, locations, movements, or custody when their existing semantics
match. Those producers own the logistical facts.

`@refinio/biocontamination.core` owns whether an item is qualified for a
method, whether calibration or verification is current, whether sample custody
is valid, and what a result means under the approved quality program. It shall
reference supply facts rather than copying them, and it shall not treat a
commercial custody transfer as laboratory sample custody unless the typed
contract proves the same event. Aggregat may consume or produce supply facts;
it does not own EN 17141 quality decisions.

### Storage and distribution

Each quality-domain producer writes to a program-scoped biocontamination trie:

`biocontamination:<ownerPerson>:<ownerInstance>:<programId>:v1`

The root is a stable `BiocontaminationTrieRoot` identity and the sole IdAccess
sharing boundary for that producer's program evidence. UVC intervention
evidence remains under its UVC-owned root and connects through exact typed
references. Do not create per-entry or per-child Access grants.

Every biocontamination entry except the `BiocontaminationProgram` root itself
shall include `program: SHA256IdHash<BiocontaminationProgram>` so entries from
different programs cannot be merged by a UI or query.

The trie recipe-declared graph is the synchronization boundary. Exact evidence
references shall be present when the producer writes the entry. Import
handlers shall wake the owning `BiocontaminationPlan` from concrete semantic
imports. They shall not create timestamped copies, scan storage, or infer
missing lineage.

## ONE object rules

All new types shall follow these invariants:

- `$type$`, interface name, recipe name, ambient declaration, and allowed type
  names match exactly.
- New biocontamination and UVC-intervention types use `$version$: 'v1'`.
- Versioned identities use compact stable `isId: true` fields.
- `SHA256IdHash<T>` and `referenceToId` represent logical/versioned identities.
- `SHA256Hash<T>` and `referenceToObj` represent exact revisions or immutable
  evidence.
- Creator functions reject missing, malformed, contradictory, or temporally
  impossible input.
- Required facts never receive `Unknown`, zero, current-time, or generic
  fallback values.
- Recipe rule order is treated as serialized object order.
- Recipes and reverse maps are registered before any read or write.
- A MultiUser runtime supplying custom recipes shall provide the complete
  one.models plus UVC aggregate, not a partial compliance-only list.
- Producer-owned provenance is stored at creation time.
- Content-addressed idempotence is used directly; no `alreadyStored` cache or
  duplicate timestamped import journal is added.

### Approval object

`BiocontaminationApproval` shall contain:

- `program`
- `subject: SHA256Hash<approved object version>`
- `decision: approved | rejected | withdrawn`
- `scope`
- `approverPersonId`
- `approverInstanceId`
- `qualificationRevision`
- `decidedAt`
- `signature: SHA256Hash<Signature>`

Approval is valid only when:

- the subject hash is exact;
- the approval signature verifies;
- the approver qualification covered the scope at `decidedAt`;
- the subject had not been superseded or withdrawn before its effective use.

Editing an approved version creates a new unapproved version. Approval never
floats from one version to another.

- `sampleId`
- `program`

- exact risk assessment revision;
- exact location version;
- exact sampling method revision;
- exact effective limit-set revision;
- scheduled event reference;
- environment operating state;

- collection start/end;
- equipment qualification, calibration, and verification refs;

- exact sample hash;

- from/to custodian when applicable;
- Person/Instance producer;
- time;
- location;
- condition observations;

Custody is append-only. A later event does not edit or replace an earlier

- sample hash;
- medium batch and suitability evidence;
- incubation equipment and verification;
- incubation conditions and times;
- count/read observations;
- isolate or identification observations;
- analyst identity and qualification;
- invalidity or interference observations.

`BiocontaminationResult` contains:

- exact sample and observation refs;
- result measure;
- numeric value or validated qualitative result;
- unit;
- organism identification refs where applicable;
- calculation/method revision;
- laboratory producer identity;
- result time;
- review state.

The result creator shall not accept generic device telemetry as a

- program and controlled environment;
- exact applicability declaration;
- exact strategy and risk-assessment revisions;
- exact approved treatment procedure;
- exact room version and resource descriptions;
- target surfaces/volumes and organism scope;
- target dose/coverage specification;
- required safety observations;
- required operator qualification;
- scheduled time and validity window;
- authorization/approval.

`UvcInterventionExecution` is immutable and produced at termination. It

- exact intervention-plan version;
- exact terminal `UvcDisinfectionRun` version;
- exact start/stop `UvcControlObservation` refs;
- exact safety observations;
- exact dose observations;
- configuration snapshot;
- operator and controlling peer identity;
- start/end;
- termination reason;
- deviation refs;
- `cycleState: executed | failed | cancelled`.

The execution object must not contain `microbiologicallyAcceptable`,
`disinfected`, `sterile`, or `compliant`.

`UvcDoseObservation` contains:

- exact intervention plan;
- device and sensor identity;
- wavelength or spectral specification;
- irradiance and integrated dose values with units;
- spatial monitoring location/target;
- observation start/end;
- exact calibration and equipment-verification refs;
- producer Person/Instance;
- uncertainty or validity statement.

The existing optional `UvcDisinfectionRun.uvcDoseMjCm2` is legacy/display data.
It shall not be used for physical-delivery or compliance assessment unless an
exact set of valid `UvcDoseObservation` objects supports the value.

`UvcPhysicalDeliveryAssessment` references:

- execution;
- exact procedure and dose/coverage criteria;
- every dose, safety, configuration, and deviation observation reviewed;
- assessor identity and qualification;
- decision;
- rationale;
- assessment time;
- signature.

`BiocontaminationInterventionEffectivenessAssessment` references:

- execution;
- approved effectiveness protocol;
- exact pre/post or other applicable samples and microbiological results;
- result evaluations and excursions;
- qualified microbiologist;
- decision and rationale;
- assessment time;
- signature.

Physical delivery and microbiological effectiveness remain independent
assessments.

### Existing run storage contract

`UvcFacilityPlan` currently discards the `{hash, idHash}` returned by
`storeVersionedObject` and returns only the loaded `UvcDisinfectionRun`. The
UVC intervention producer must not recompute or rediscover the terminal version
hash.

The facility transition API shall therefore return a stored-version result:

- `run: UvcDisinfectionRun`
- `hash: SHA256Hash<UvcDisinfectionRun>`
- `idHash: SHA256IdHash<UvcDisinfectionRun>`

for schedule, start, complete, and fail transitions. Existing UI adapters may
continue projecting only `run`, but `UvcInterventionService` shall consume the
exact storage-returned hash when it creates `UvcInterventionExecution`.

The plan shall never obtain terminal evidence through a later latest-version
lookup because a newer correction or failure transition could then be bound to
the wrong execution.

## Required logic

### Applicability resolution

Before showing EN 17141 workflows for an environment, the plan shall resolve:

1. the environment's stable identity;
2. an approved applicability declaration for the configured standard edition;
3. its effective period at the requested activity time;
4. its exact approved strategy revision;
5. required approval and authority evidence.

Missing, expired, overlapping, or contradictory declarations produce
`incomplete-applicability` and block controlled compliance operations.

### Effective revision binding

At plan, sample, or observation creation time, the producer shall bind the
exact revisions that apply. Later reads shall use these stored refs.

The system shall not look up today's current plan, method, procedure, limit, or
qualification to reinterpret historical evidence.

### Intervention authorization

An intervention may be started only when:

- applicability is effective;
- strategy, risk assessment, and procedure revisions are approved/effective;
- the room belongs to the declared controlled environment;
- the selected resources match the approved configuration;
- the operator has a current qualification for the procedure/environment;
- all required pre-start safety observations exist and pass;
- required calibration, equipment verification, and maintenance evidence is
  current;
- no unresolved blocking change, excursion, or equipment status applies.

Failure shall create an attributable rejected-attempt record without energizing
the hardware.

### Intervention execution

Execution uses the existing `UvcControlPlan` and readback rules.

- Commanded state and observed state remain separate.
- Every enabled treatment resource requires matching start observation.
- Any partial start causes verified rollback; rollback failure is a failed
  execution and safety event.
- Safety observations are evaluated continuously where the approved procedure
  requires it.
- A safety violation invokes the approved stop path and records the reason.
- Every treatment resource requires matching stop observation.
- A terminal execution object is written only after all available evidence is
  bound.
- Device-cycle success remains `executed`, never `disinfection completed`.

### Physical-delivery evaluation

The evaluator shall use only calibrated, in-scope dose observations bound to
the execution and exact procedure criteria.

Missing target locations, expired calibration, unsupported wavelengths,
insufficient coverage, unit incompatibility, interruptions, or contradictory
observations produce `inconclusive` or `deviates-from-procedure`, never a
default pass.

### Sampling workflow

Sample creation requires:

- effective approved monitoring plan;
- scheduled/ad-hoc reason;
- exact location, method, limit set, and operating state;
- qualified collector;
- valid equipment state;
- unique sample id within the program.

Collection, transport, receipt, incubation, analysis, review, and disposal are
append-only custody/observation events. The logic shall detect missing sequence
steps, duplicate terminal events, time reversal, expired holding time, and
condition deviations.

### Result evaluation

Each result is evaluated against the exact limit-set revision stored on the
sample.

Evaluation shall:

- validate method, location, state, unit, and organism scope;
- preserve raw value and comparison;
- evaluate target, alert, action, and specification limits independently;
- create an immutable `BiocontaminationResultEvaluation`;
- open or link the required excursion;
- never overwrite the raw result;
- never silently substitute a newer or more permissive limit.

### Excursion and OOS workflow

An alert/action/specification breach or invalid monitoring condition creates or
links an excursion.

Closure requires:

- referenced triggering evidence;
- scope and impact assessment;
- immediate containment where required;
- investigation with root-cause conclusion or explicit unresolved conclusion;
- CAPA when required;
- effectiveness check;
- review of related results and product/process impact;
- qualified closure authority and signed approval.

Missing root cause is not replaced with a generic cause. An inconclusive
investigation remains visible.

### Trending

Trend inputs must be homogeneous or explicitly stratified by:

- program/environment;
- monitoring location version;
- method revision;
- sample type;
- operating state;
- organism/result measure;
- unit;
- limit-set revision and effective period.

The trend review records its algorithm/method, time interval, complete input
result refs, exclusions with rationale, findings, reviewer qualification,
decision, and signature.

The UI shall not merge incompatible methods or environments into one line.

### Periodic verification

Periodic program review references:

- current and superseded applicability/strategy/risk/plan/limit revisions;
- environmental monitoring results and trends;
- product/process contamination information where supplied;
- interventions and effectiveness assessments;
- excursions, investigations, CAPAs, and effectiveness checks;
- training/qualification state;
- changes and open actions.

The output is a qualified decision with evidence, limitations, and follow-up
actions. It is not a global compliance flag.

### Claim projection

Journal and dashboard projections shall render:

- `UVC cycle executed — device state verified`;
- physical-delivery assessment separately;
- microbiological assessment separately;
- applicable program/environment/standard edition;
- exact evidence count by evidence class;
- missing or invalid evidence;
- open excursions and review state.

Replace:

- `Disinfection completed` with `UVC cycle executed`;
- `verified observations` with `verified device readbacks` when the evidence
  consists only of `UvcControlObservation`;
- generic `Completed` with the state family being displayed.

The words `sterile`, `microbiologically acceptable`, `effective
decontamination`, and `EN 17141 conforming` require their own authorized
assessment evidence and shall never be inferred from `UvcDisinfectionRun`.

   `@refinio/biocontamination.core`; keep UVC intervention recipes under

## Migration and compatibility

- Existing `UvcDisinfectionRun` records remain valid device-cycle records.
- Existing records are never rewritten or silently upgraded.
- A legacy run can participate in a new evidence graph only through a new
  explicit `UvcInterventionExecution` that references its exact terminal
  version and supplies the missing program/procedure/context evidence.
- If required historical context was not captured at execution time, the
  execution remains `incomplete`; current master data shall not be backfilled
  as though it were historical fact.
- Existing Journal records shall be relabeled to device-cycle terminology.
- The old optional dose field remains display-only until supported by exact
  calibrated dose observations.
- Demo entries shall never appear in compliance projections or exports.

## Security, safety, and integrity requirements

- Fail closed when identity, authorization, qualification, approval, method,
  calibration, safety state, or provenance is missing.
- Never energize UV-C based only on a UI confirmation that a room is empty.
- Required hardware interlock, access-control, occupancy, door, motion, and
  emergency-stop states shall be producer-owned observations when required by
  the approved procedure.
- Never log secrets, private keys, laboratory credentials, or signing material.
- Never allow an observation producer to claim another Person/Instance.
- Never treat mDNS discovery as device identity or authority.
- Never automatically retry physical writes.
- Never alter raw samples, observations, results, approvals, or signatures.
- Corrections are new linked objects with reason, author, and time.
- Contradictory evidence remains visible.
- All exports include object hashes, id hashes where applicable, recipe
  versions, signatures, and the exact evidence graph used by each conclusion.

## Validation and test requirements

### Recipe tests

For every new object:

- construct a valid instance;
- reject missing identity and required refs;
- reject malformed timestamps, units, ranges, and state combinations;
- register and store/read using the correct versioned or unversioned API;
- confirm deterministic id hashes for versioned identities;
- confirm recipe child discovery includes all exact provenance refs.

### Plan tests

- applicability cannot be inferred from room kind;
- unapproved or expired revisions block controlled actions;
- edited approved objects require a new approval;
- historical samples retain historical method/limit revisions;
- unqualified people cannot collect, approve, review, or close;
- device start requires safety and procedure preconditions;
- partial start rolls back and records failure;
- completed hardware readback creates `executed`, not microbiological success;
- raw telemetry cannot be stored as a microbiological result;
- uncalibrated dose cannot pass physical-delivery assessment;
- incompatible units or methods cannot be compared silently;
- limit breaches open the correct excursion class;
- an excursion cannot close without required investigation/effectiveness
  evidence;
- trend inputs cannot cross incompatible scopes without explicit
  stratification;
- missing evidence produces incomplete/inconclusive state, not a default.

### Distribution tests

- sharing grants only the program-scoped biocontamination trie id root;
- CHUM recursively transfers exact referenced evidence;
- semantic imports wake `BiocontaminationPlan`;
- repeated import is content-addressed and creates no duplicate events;
- revoked access removes the program root from the sharing surface;
- no projection depends on startup scans or polling after live imports.

### Physical validation tests

Automated tests use fakes by default. Explicit commissioning protocols shall
verify:

- authenticated device identity;
- safe start and emergency stop;
- hardware interlocks and exclusion controls;
- read-after-write;
- sensor identity and calibration;
- wavelength, irradiance, dose integration, and spatial coverage;
- interruption and power-loss behavior;
- clock synchronization and timestamp provenance;
- evidence export and independent trace verification.

Physical execution evidence alone does not make the microbiological-control
tests pass.

## Acceptance criteria

The first compliance-capable release is accepted only when:

- a facility can create an explicit applicable or non-applicable declaration
  for a controlled environment;
- approved program, strategy, risk, monitoring plan, limits, procedure, and
  qualification revisions are typed and traceable;
- a UVC intervention is blocked when required authority, qualification,
  safety, calibration, or procedure evidence is absent;
- an execution binds exact device, safety, configuration, and dose evidence;
- the UI labels it as a UVC cycle, not completed disinfection;
- samples preserve exact plan, location, method, limits, collector, equipment,
  and custody evidence;
- microbiological results preserve raw observations and qualified review;
- result evaluation uses the sample-bound limit revision;
- excursions, OOS, investigations, CAPAs, effectiveness checks, and closure are
  traceable;
- trends preserve compatible scope and complete input lineage;
- periodic review can produce a signed evidence trace with limitations;
- no single `compliant` field exists;
- no device telemetry is accepted as viable microbiological evidence;
- no legacy run is silently promoted into EN 17141 evidence;
- recipe registration, storage, CHUM propagation, and domain-plan tests pass on
  every runtime consuming the graph.

## Delivery sequence

### Phase 1 — Claim correction and program authority

- Correct Journal terminology.
- Add program, environment, applicability, strategy, approval, qualification,
  and controlled-document objects.
- Add the biocontamination trie, plan, registration, sharing, and evidence
  trace.

### Phase 2 — Risk, monitoring plan, methods, and limits

- Add sources/routes, microorganisms, risk assessments, monitoring locations,
  schedules, method revisions, equipment evidence, and limit sets.
- Implement effective-revision and qualification resolution.

### Phase 3 — Controlled UVC intervention

- Add intervention plan/execution, safety, dose, configuration, deviation,
  physical-delivery assessment, and legacy-run association.
- Enforce pre-start and continuous safety logic.

### Phase 4 — Microbiological evidence

- Add samples, custody, observations, results, evaluations, and laboratory
  producer integration.
- Implement exact limit evaluation and excursion creation.

### Phase 5 — Investigation, CAPA, trends, and periodic review

- Add investigations, containment, CAPA, effectiveness checks, trend reviews,
  change control, and periodic program verification.
- Deliver signed trace export and auditor projection.

## Definition of done

The feature is done only when the code, recipes, tests, UI vocabulary, evidence
trace, and operational documentation all preserve the central boundary:

> Device authority proves device observations. Calibrated physical evidence
> supports physical-delivery assessment. Valid microbiological observations
> support microbiological assessment. Qualified facility authority determines
> program decisions. None of these meanings substitutes for another.
