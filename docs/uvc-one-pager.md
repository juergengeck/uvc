# UVC.one

## Certified disinfection, delivered as a service

UVC.one turns UV-C disinfection from an equipment event into a documented service. For each room and cycle, the service connects an approved procedure, authorized devices and operators, observed UV-C output, safety state, and a reviewable result. Independent devices record what they actually observed, so a facility can trace a completed cycle back to its source evidence instead of relying on a handwritten log or a lamp's “on” signal.

**The certificate is the service outcome:** a signed, attributable record that a defined disinfection procedure was performed and assessed against its approved criteria. A cycle that is interrupted, lacks evidence, or falls outside those criteria remains visibly incomplete or failed. Biological effectiveness and regulatory compliance require their own validation, measurements, and responsible approval; a device record alone does not establish either.

## Detailed metering comes as a side effect

The same evidence collected to certify the service also provides fine-grained metering. Each cycle can be accounted for by room, device, time, duration, measured irradiance or dose where sensors support it, interruptions, and outcome. Facilities gain a practical basis for utilization reports, maintenance decisions, service-level reporting, and usage-based billing without asking staff to create a second record.

## How it works

1. **Define the service:** Link a room and device configuration to an approved UV-C procedure and its acceptance criteria.
2. **Observe the run:** The light source and independent sensors capture timestamped observations; a facility-owned witness keeps the record under the facility's control.
3. **Assess and attest:** Authorized reviewers assess the evidence. Signed attestations preserve device identity, provenance, exceptions, and the resulting decision.
4. **Use the data:** Share a verifiable service record with the people who need assurance, and derive metering and operational reports from the same underlying observations.

**UVC.one makes every UV-C service accountable: evidence for the disinfection claim, and precise usage data generated in the process.**
